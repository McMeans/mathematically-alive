Color

One meaning per color. Amber is what's picked or lit. Green is a gain in odds, red a loss. Use the Tailwind token, never the hex, in a component.

Motion

Short and eased, never bouncy. A lit number flips once when it changes (.plate-hang, 180ms). The phone dock glows amber when odds move (.bump, 900ms). Reduced motion cuts both to 1ms.

Contrast

WCAG ratios computed from the tokens. Small text needs 4.5. The focus ring is a 2px amber outline, offset 2px.

Known gap: the amber focus ring is 1.5:1 on ground and 1.8:1 on paper, under the 3:1 WCAG asks of focus indicators. It reads well on the board.

The mark

A football whose laces form a pulse line. Cream ball on the board, board-green ball on light grounds. Below 48px it drops the lace ticks and thickens the pulse.

src/app/Logo.tsx: defaults to the cream ball and green pulse; pass color/pulse on light grounds; decorative hides it from screen readers when the name is beside it.

src/app/icon.svg (favicon, simple form) and public/logo.svg (full form) are the green ball with a cream pulse.
