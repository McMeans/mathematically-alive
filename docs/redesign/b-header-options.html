Team header: odds band, refined

Option B, with one question worked three ways: where the playoff's second-tier numbers go. Playoff, title game and conference title answer "is my team alive"; first-round bye and national title matter, but less. Today they read as a sentence of prose under 17%. In all three below they become small lit numbers (the board's own language, not body text), a clear step down in size and color from the band's three. Each is shown for a long shot (Liberty, today's data) and a contender (made-up numbers, to show the tier when the values are big), at desktop 1180, narrow or short desktop 820, and phone 375.

Same in all three: the identity row (picker, record, tags, actions at the right; under 1000px the actions take their own row, never wrapping into the numbers), and the band of three equal cells, label over lit number. On phones the band is the sticky row under the name line.

B1 · Sub-scores in the playoff cell

Closest to what the numbers mean. Bye and national title are parts of the playoff answer, so they sit inside its cell, under the big number, as two small label-and-number pairs (17px against 44px, board-text against white). Short labels ("Bye", "Natl title") keep them on one line down to phone width. The playoff cell runs a little taller than its neighbours, which quietly marks it as the lead.

B2 · A side column at the band's end

Out of the main row entirely. A narrow fourth column after a dashed rule, headed "If in the field", lists both as a two-line mini table. The three cells stay perfectly even; the pair reads as an appendix. On phones it folds into one dashed row under the band.

B3 · A foot line under the band

Quietest. One thin row under the band, "Playoff road", both numbers small with full labels; nothing shares a cell with the big three. Reads like a scoreboard's fine print. Costs one more thin row (about 28px) and is the easiest to overlook.

Edge cases, the same in each: a four-team field has no byes (only National title shows); once the field is announced the pair becomes Seed and National title ("Won", "Out" or odds); an independent's band has one main cell (Playoff) and the pair keeps its tier. Short labels ("Bye", "Natl title") would come from the core beside today's playoffDetail.
