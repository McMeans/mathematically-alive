Group of Six race · draft 1

In 2026 the highest-ranked Group of Six team gets a bid, conference champion or not. Every other Group of Six team needs one of the seven at-large spots, like anyone else. Most years that's one Group of Six team, seeded 12 under the line. But a second (or third) can get in at large, and a Group of Six team's real race is usually against the other Group of Six teams, not the at-large bubble.

The card here is the one we built (layout C: seed, team, projected rank, field odds). Pick a scenario, a layout, and how the G6 tag works.

Rules in the draft

  • The bid: the Group of Six team with the best projected final rank. It counts as an automatic bid even when it would have made the field at large, so a second Group of Six team never takes the bid's spot; it takes an at-large one.
  • G6 tag, always: on the bid team wherever it's seeded (what we built). Only when needed: like Notre Dame's ND tag, only when the bid is what gets it in, that is, when it's ranked below the last at-large team.
  • At-large Group of Six teams get no tag: they're in like any other at-large team.
  • "Next for the G6 bid" / the Group of Six block only shows on a Group of Six team's page. Power and independent pages get the card as built.
