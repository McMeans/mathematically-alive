Team header odds: sized to the team

The odds band filled the width whatever a team has, so an independent (Notre Dame, UConn) got one number in a long empty strip. Three fresh layouts, each sized to what the team has: three numbers and the playoff road for a conference team, one number (and the road, if any) for an independent. Same identity row, buttons and phone name line in all three.

Real numbers from today: Liberty 17% · In · 72%, road <0.1% / <0.1%. Notre Dame 99%, road 51% / 19%. UConn <0.1%, no road.

O1 · Scoreline

One line, like a scoreboard's line score. Each label sits beside its number; hairlines between, the playoff road after a dashed rule in small numbers. No fill and no strip: the line is only as long as what it says. The flattest and most compact on desktop (one 56px row). On phones labels go back over the numbers so three fit across, and the road takes a thin line under them.

O2 · In the name row

No extra row. The numbers join the picker's row on the right, top-aligned with it, Playoff biggest with its road stacked under it ("Bye", "Natl title"). Record, tags and buttons take the row under. Most compact overall and the numbers are the first thing beside the name; the cost is a busier top row, and long names (Mississippi State, Florida International) push the numbers to wrap under the name below about 1000px.

O3 · Fitted band

Today's band, sized to fit. The dark band hugs its cells and sits left-aligned under the identity row; the road folds in as its last, smaller cell after a dashed rule. Keeps the band's "this is the scoreboard" weight, and an independent gets one short block, not a strip. On phones the band spans the width as now (three cells, or Playoff plus the road).

All three keep the playoff road a clear step down from the main numbers, and drop it when there's none (UConn). Numbers stay lit digits on the board; nothing new in the type scale beyond the sizes shown.
