Rules in the draft

  • Projected final rank: the team's median final rank over the simulated seasons, after championship week, with the page's picks locked. Past 25 it's our stand-in's order, not the committee's, but it still places a far-off team (Virginia 70).
  • Field: the four power champions, the highest-ranked Group of Six team, Notre Dame if projected 12th or better, then at-large by projected rank to 12. Seeds straight by projected rank.
  • Tags: every power champion and the Group of Six team, always. Notre Dame only when the top-12 rule is what gets it in. Edge: a tagged team seeded under the at-large line.
  • Window: well inside the field, two either side. Near the line or out: the two above the line, every bid under it, the first teams out (down to the page's team if close), ⋯ then the team if far.
